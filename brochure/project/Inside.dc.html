SeaLease

Shipping containers you can own outright.

SeaLease lets you buy whole shipping containers under an investment plan. Each container is assigned to you by number and leased out for the plan's tenure, while you are paid every month.

HOW IT WORKS

Four steps from plan to payout.

1
Choose a plan
Pick the container price and tenure that suit you.
2
Pay and get your number
Once paid, a container is assigned to you by its container number.
3
Your container is leased
It is leased out for the plan's full tenure.
4
Get paid every month
Rent plus 100 ÷ tenure % of the price, until the full price is back.

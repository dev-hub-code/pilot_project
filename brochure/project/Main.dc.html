WHY A CONTAINER

A real asset with your number on it.

You own the whole box
Once your payment clears, a container is assigned to you by its container number.
Leased for a fixed tenure
Your container is leased out for the plan's tenure, so the end date is known from day one.
Your price comes back
Part of the price is returned every month — the whole price by the end of the tenure.
EVERY MONTH

Two amounts, paid to you monthly.

Monthly rent
The plan's rent on your container, for every month of the tenure.
+
Price return
100 ÷ tenure % of your container's price, so the full price is back by the last month.
EXAMPLE · 20-MONTH PLAN
100 ÷ 20 = 5% of the price comes back each month, plus rent.
20 monthly installments = 100% of the price
Rent and tenure differ by plan — see the plans inside.
